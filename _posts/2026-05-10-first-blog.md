Welcome to the first post on my blog.

I have wanted to create a space like this for a long time: a small corner where I can regularly write down what I am thinking about, what I am learning, and what I am still confused by. Research moves fast, work moves even faster, and many interesting ideas disappear simply because we never pause long enough to turn them into words. This blog is my way of slowing down just enough to keep those ideas.

I will mostly write about four themes here.

**AI**  
This will probably be the core of the blog. I spend a lot of time thinking about LLM agents, memory, retrieval, reasoning, and what makes AI systems actually useful beyond demos. Some posts may be technical, some may be reflective, and some may just be notes about ideas that are still half-formed.

**Finance**  
I am also deeply interested in financial intelligence, market narratives, and how AI can help people understand complex financial information. I expect to write about research problems, product ideas, benchmark results, and occasionally broader thoughts on how technology is changing the way we read markets.

**Football**  
Not everything valuable has to be formal research. Football is one of the best examples I know of strategy, emotion, uncertainty, and momentum all interacting in real time. I may write about matches, teams, tactics, or simply why the sport continues to matter to me. I am also a big fan of Manchester City and Argentina.

**Life**  
Some posts will not fit neatly into any professional category. They may be about studying, building, ambition, uncertainty, growing up, or just the small observations that stay with me longer than expected. I want this blog to leave room for those thoughts too.

I do not expect every post here to be polished or definitive. Some will be careful analyses, while others may be closer to working notes from an ongoing journey. But I hope all of them will be honest. If a blog is worth keeping, I think it should reflect not only what someone knows, but also what they are still trying to understand.

So this is the beginning.  
I will keep updating this space with thoughts on AI, finance, football, life, and whatever else feels worth thinking through carefully.

Thanks for reading.
