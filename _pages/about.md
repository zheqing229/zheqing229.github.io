I am a student at the Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence, Renmin University of China, and I have been admitted to the direct-entry PhD program. My research focuses on **LLM Agents**, **Deep Research**, and **AI4Finance**.

In research, I have published at [ACL 2025 (Oral)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12450) and [ACL Findings 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.02845v1), covering multi-agent social simulation and long-horizon conversational memory. In industry, I helped develop **BullClaw**, a financial report generation system that ranked **3rd overall and 1st in the U.S. market** on the [OpenFinArena](https://openfinarena.com/fin-doc-research) benchmark.

I am proficient in LangGraph and other agent frameworks, with extensive experience using Claude Code, Codex, and other AI agents. I am also familiar with concepts such as Skills, Harness, Memory, and RAG.

For more information, visit my [playground](/playground/).
